How to Play

FrostBound Bots

  1. Choose a robot

    Click or tap a robot on the board or in the list, or press 1–4.

  2. Choose a direction

    Use the arrow keys or the on-screen arrows. Robots slide until they hit a wall, the edge of the board, or another robot. Use the other robots to help the target robot stop on its target tile.

  3. Stop on the target tile

    Crossing the target tile does not win the round. Minimum Moves is the fewest slides needed from the starting positions.

Hint shows the first move of a shortest solution from your current position. Solve finds and plays the whole sequence. Undo takes back one move. Restart Round (R) restores the starting positions for this round.

Next Round keeps the board and final robot positions and chooses a new objective. New Game starts a chapter with a new layout.
